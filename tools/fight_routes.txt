# The route starts from the movie's save and chooses its commands by game
# state, so whether the fight is won (or lost, where the movie loses it) and
# where play ends up don't hang on the frames a recorded press meets. The gate
# runs these routes (tools/suite.py); tools/outcomes.py then stops pinning the
# movie's exact places, saved game and save RAM, and keeps its protected
# matches. A movie's own route is named in its header, or, for a hand recording, the route
# names the movie. A carried route fails
# where one of its fights needs more retries than its bound (none unless the
# line gives one, with its reason), or where it finishes with other facts than
# the movie's reference end state (tools/fight_facts.txt): levels, experience,
# gold, the items won and the story's event memory.
