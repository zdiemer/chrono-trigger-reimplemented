# Sun Stone's reference play, after --drifted calibration over events-sun-stone:
#
# One per line: entry limit reason...
#   entry   a hand port's name, as in game/registry.c
#   limit   drift, vblank-tail, vblank-worst or worst
#   reason  why the error doesn't reach the budget the limit stands for (required)

#
# Each of these is an entry whose time follows its data in a way one scalar
# can't, and whose error was shown not to reach a budget that matters: the
# vblank check (tools/vblank_check.py) finds none of their writes moved. A port
# whose time follows which other routines it runs inline attributes their part
# to them instead (NATIVE_TIME_AS; docs/REIMPLEMENTATION.md, "The timing gate",
# "Dispatchers"), which is what emptied this list of palette_animate and the
# fades: what is left follows a path inside one routine, or a loop of the
# port's own. The vblank limits judge only the stretches the original began
# and ended within one vblank, which emptied it of the calls that begin in
# vblank and outlast it (loads, setups, the load screen's frame): their error
# moves work on the picture, and `worst` judges it there.


# The decompressor, which has no entry since the data subsystem's teardown:
# its estimate, as its entries made it (charge_retired), is charged inside its
# callers' stretches, which carry its error. A call is 260-290 lines on
# average (a frame is 262), seven times the length of vblank, and interrupts
# are taken during its time: its error (6% on average, up to 333k clocks, on
# this list while it had entries) moves the end of a load, not a video write.
# The callers below carried it under the unpacker's own entry until then (its
# time was a part tagged as that entry's, or a call-out to it), so the error
# moved, not grew: their calibrations, far from their rates or near 1, show
# their own work is not what is off.
