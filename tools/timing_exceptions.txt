# Accepted over-limit entries of the timing gate (tools/timing_gate.py;
# docs/RECOMP.md, "The timing gate").
#
# One per line: entry limit reason...
#   entry   a hand port's name, as in game/registry.c
#   limit   drift, vblank-tail, vblank-worst or worst
#   reason  why the error doesn't reach the budget the limit stands for (required)
#
# Each of these is an entry whose time follows its data in a way one scalar
# can't, and whose error was shown not to reach a budget that matters: the
# vblank check (tools/vblank_check.py) finds none of their writes moved. A port
# whose time follows which other routines it runs inline attributes their part
# to them instead (NATIVE_TIME_AS; docs/RECOMP.md, "The timing gate",
# "Dispatchers"), which is what emptied this list of palette_animate and the
# fades: what is left follows a path inside one routine, or a loop of the
# port's own. The vblank limits judge only the stretches the original began
# and ended within one vblank, which emptied it of the calls that begin in
# vblank and outlast it (loads, setups, the load screen's frame): their error
# moves work on the picture, and `worst` judges it there.

# The decompressor: a call is 260-290 lines on average (a frame is 262),
# seven times the length of vblank, and interrupts are taken during its time: its error
# moves the end of a load (by 6% of it on average), not a video write. Its time
# follows the compressed data, literals against copies of every length.
decompress worst a load 7 vblanks long on average, 6% error; its error moves the end of the load, a frame sooner or later, not a video write
decompress_direct worst the decompressor writing straight to memory: as decompress

# Main-loop work that runs on into vblank after the NMI handler, writing RAM
# (tables and tile buffers the next frame uploads), not video registers: its
# error moves when the RAM is ready, and the calls are as long as vblank or
# longer, so the original's own time already runs them into the picture.
field_rasterize_quad worst rasterizes a window shape into a RAM table, 7.5% error: the original steps each pixel of an edge's run and the port each line, so the error follows the shape's widths; at most 80899 clocks, in calls up to four times vblank's length, and no video write moves
field_move_screen_effect_on worst the searchlight's frames draw its shape as field_rasterize_quad, timed as it (TIMED_AS) and off by what it is off by; at most 75000 clocks, writing RAM tables, and no video write moves
field_unpack_tile_maps worst unpacks a map's tile layers and attributes at its load, writing RAM; its time follows the runs in the stream: at most 157575 clocks in a call of 658247 on average, moving the end of the load
menu_draw_glyph vblank-tail draws a glyph into the menu's tile buffer in RAM in 18-line calls; its pixels are attributed to $C2:5E89, whose own time follows the pixel's bits (7.1% on its own); at most 2932 clocks (two lines)
menu_draw_text vblank-tail draws text into the menu's tilemap buffer in RAM in 19-line calls; its tiles and numbers are attributed (19% of vblank calls off by a line, from 32%), the control codes' handlers are no entries and the loop follows the string; at most 3498 clocks (2.6 lines) in vblank
menu_run_window_list vblank-tail draws a window command list into the menus' tilemap buffers in RAM, which the next vblank uploads; its commands are charged at their handlers' rates and its strings at menu_draw_text's, so it inherits the text's spread: its calls in vblank are nearly all one field list (a string) off by 1040 clocks each, mean |error| 1.1% and no drift over all calls
menu_draw_sprite_nodes vblank-tail draws the sprite list into the OAM buffer in RAM, which the next vblank uploads, 40510 clocks a call on average; its time is charged per node and per sprite as the original spends it (dropped across, dropped down, shown), 2.97% of its 236 calls in vblank off by 1024 clocks or more, at most 3416 clocks (2.6 lines)
menu_read_design_and_chapter drift reads a slot's window design and chapter, the chapter label's drawing attributed to menu_draw_chapter_label (cost_milli 1); what is left over-estimates by about 3700 clocks in calls of 519000 (+0.71%), never in vblank, writing the summary into RAM the next vblank sends. Judged since the New Game+ movie took it past 100 stretches
menu_object_cmd_for_frames_moving vblank-tail runs its object's sprite script inline, whose commands (a show, a byte change, an upload queued) vary from frame to frame under one scalar; 1.54% of its calls in vblank off by 1024 clocks or more, at most 2982 clocks (2.3 lines), writing the object and the sprite list in RAM

# Starting an object: the free-slot walk looks at one slot or at sixty, and
# only the call that finds one copies the caller's state into it ($3B bytes),
# so the share of a call that is the copy swings with the walk, which one
# scalar can't fit. The spawn itself is within the limits since its
# recalibration; the palette row command, which runs a spawn, still spreads.
# It drifts by 0.01% or less and the vblank check finds none of its writes moved.
menu_object_cmd_load_palette_row vblank-tail its time is the spawn it attributes to $C2:048A, so it inherits that spread; at most 1681 clocks with -0.00% drift

# The console's interrupt vectors (core/console.c): each is one long jump to its
# RAM stub, which the port takes with no bus access of its own, so its estimate
# is 0 against the 32 clocks the jump takes; no scalar can scale nothing. It
# moves the start of every interrupt's handler by those 32 clocks, the same each
# time, and the vblank check finds none of the handlers' writes moved.
console_vblank_vector drift a long jump to the RAM stub with no access of its own: 32 clocks a call estimated as 0, the same at every vblank
console_line_vector drift as console_vblank_vector, at the line interrupt
