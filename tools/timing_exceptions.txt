# Accepted over-limit entries of the timing gate (tools/timing_gate.py;
# docs/RECOMP.md, "The timing gate").
#
# One per line: entry limit reason...
#   entry   a hand port's name, as in game/registry.c
#   limit   drift, vblank-tail, vblank-worst or worst
#   reason  why the error doesn't reach the budget the limit stands for (required)
#
# Each of these is an entry whose time follows its data in a way one scalar
# can't, and whose error was shown not to reach a budget that matters: the
# vblank check (tools/vblank_check.py) finds none of their writes moved. A port
# whose time follows which other routines it runs inline attributes their part
# to them instead (NATIVE_TIME_AS; docs/RECOMP.md, "The timing gate",
# "Dispatchers"), which is what emptied this list of palette_animate and the
# fades: what is left follows a path inside one routine, or a loop of the
# port's own.

# The decompressor: a call is 260-290 lines on average (a frame is 262),
# seven times the length of vblank, and interrupts are taken during its time: its error
# moves the end of a load (by 6% of it on average), not a video write. Its time
# follows the compressed data, literals against copies of every length.
decompress vblank-tail a load 7 vblanks long on average; its error moves the end of the load, not a video write
decompress vblank-worst as for vblank-tail: the error of a call that starts in vblank lands frames later, at the load's end
decompress worst as for vblank-tail: 6% of a 260-line call on average; a load ends a frame sooner or later
decompress_direct vblank-tail the decompressor writing straight to memory: as decompress
decompress_direct vblank-worst as decompress
decompress_direct worst as decompress

# Main-loop work that runs on into vblank after the NMI handler, writing RAM
# (tables and tile buffers the next frame uploads), not video registers: its
# error moves when the RAM is ready, and the calls are as long as vblank or
# longer, so the original's own time already runs them into the picture.
field_rasterize_quad vblank-tail rasterizes a window shape into a RAM table (35 lines a call, as long as vblank); 7.5% error, the time follows the quadrilateral's edges
field_rasterize_quad vblank-worst as for vblank-tail: at most 14885 clocks (11 lines) in a call of 35 lines or more, writing RAM
menu_object_wave_hdma vblank-tail fills a RAM scroll-wave table in 53-line calls, longer than vblank; its error never exceeds 1396 clocks (one line), just over the tail's 1024
menu_draw_glyph vblank-tail draws a glyph into the menu's tile buffer in RAM in 18-line calls; its pixels are attributed to $C2:5E89, whose own time follows the pixel's bits (7.1% on its own); at most 2932 clocks (two lines)
menu_draw_text vblank-tail draws text into the menu's tilemap buffer in RAM in 19-line calls; its tiles and numbers are attributed (19% of vblank calls off by a line, from 32%), the control codes' handlers are no entries and the loop follows the string; at most 3498 clocks (2.6 lines) in vblank

# The scene engine's circle, a RAM window table the next frame's HDMA reads.
scene_draw_circle vblank-tail draws a circle into a RAM window table in 28-line calls; its time follows the radius (the midpoint steps and the fills); at most 1883 clocks (1.4 lines)
scene_draw_circle_jump vblank-tail the same routine through its jump at $C3:0008: as scene_draw_circle
battle_draw_objects vblank-tail draws the battle objects into the OAM buffer in RAM in 37-line calls, longer than vblank; the routines it runs per object are attributed (10.7% error to 3.7%), what is left is its own draw list build and exchange sort, whose swaps follow the order; at most 7311 clocks (5.4 lines)
battle_draw_object_underlays vblank-tail part of battle_draw_objects' pass, into the OAM buffer in RAM; its marker, number sprites and effect sprites are attributed (55.4% error to 12.7%), what is left is its own loop over the object's eight effect records, whose time follows which are set; at most 1855 clocks
battle_step_effect_slots vblank-tail steps the 19 effect slots' paths in RAM; its handlers and their helpers are attributed (18.1% error to 4.9%, 7.9% of vblank calls off by a line to 1.9%), what is left follows its own loop over which slots are active and due, and the handlers' own spread; at most 2838 clocks, writing RAM the object drawing reads later in the frame
battle_screen_wave_windows vblank-tail fills the two window line buffers in RAM in 94-line calls; 1.3% error, at most 4506 clocks
battle_step_hp_gradient vblank-tail writes three palette rows in RAM, uploaded at the next vblank; 1.58% of calls off by 1024 clocks or more, at most 2509

# The Epoch's time dial, which rebuilds a per-line window table in RAM that the
# next frame's HDMA reads: the vblank check finds none of its writes moved.
menu_epoch_build_gate_window vblank-tail rebuilds the gate's per-line window table in RAM in 250-line calls, longer than vblank; its time follows the triangle (how many lines its corners span and which edges cross each), 4.7% on average and at most 5864 clocks (22 lines) of a 250-line call
menu_epoch_time_dial_object vblank-tail the object that drives the dial: the window rebuild is attributed to $C2:6CC1, what is left is a setup call that moves two 448-byte buffers and programs the slot against 946 calls that turn the dial or do nothing, which one scalar can't fit
menu_epoch_time_dial_object vblank-worst as for vblank-tail: the setup call, once per screen, writing the buffers the next frame's HDMA reads

# Starting an object: the free-slot walk looks at one slot or at sixty, and
# only the call that finds one copies the caller's state into it ($3B bytes),
# so the share of a call that is the copy swings with the walk, which one
# scalar can't fit. Both entries drift by 0.01% or less over 14 movies and the
# vblank check finds none of their writes moved.
menu_object_spawn vblank-tail the walk is 1 to 60 slots and only the call that finds one copies the state; at most 1574 clocks (1.2 lines) with +0.01% drift, and the copy writes a slot, not a video register
menu_object_cmd_load_palette_row vblank-tail its time is the spawn it attributes to $C2:048A, so it inherits that spread; at most 1681 clocks with -0.00% drift

# Scrolling the menus' map: the call moves as many tiles as the frame's move
# covered, and only the moves that cross a tile boundary build a strip of the
# tilemap and queue it, so a pass is a twentieth of a pass that builds one and
# the mix decides the call. Both write work RAM only -- the strip goes into the
# upload buffer and a record into the queue the next vblank empties -- and the
# vblank check finds none of their writes moved.
menu_map_scroll_x vblank-tail builds a tilemap strip into the RAM upload buffer on the moves that cross a tile boundary and nothing on the others; at most 1445 clocks (1.1 lines) with +0.14% drift, writing RAM the next vblank uploads
menu_map_scroll_y vblank-tail as for menu_map_scroll_x, with two records a strip; at most 1346 clocks with +0.13% drift
