# Accepted over-limit entries of the timing gate (tools/timing_gate.py;
# docs/RECOMP.md, "The timing gate").
#
# One per line: entry limit reason...
#   entry   a hand port's name, as in game/registry.c
#   limit   drift, vblank-tail, vblank-worst or worst
#   reason  why the error doesn't reach the budget the limit stands for (required)
#
# Each of these is an entry whose time follows its data in a way one scalar
# can't, and whose error was shown not to reach a budget that matters: the
# vblank check (tools/vblank_check.py) finds none of their writes moved.

# The decompressor: a call is 260-290 lines on average (a frame is 262),
# seven times the length of vblank, and interrupts are taken during its time: its error
# moves the end of a load (by 6% of it on average), not a video write. Its time
# follows the compressed data, literals against copies of every length.
decompress vblank-tail a load 7 vblanks long on average; its error moves the end of the load, not a video write
decompress vblank-worst as for vblank-tail: the error of a call that starts in vblank lands frames later, at the load's end
decompress worst as for vblank-tail: 6% of a 260-line call on average; a load ends a frame sooner or later
decompress_direct vblank-tail the decompressor writing straight to memory: as decompress
decompress_direct vblank-worst as decompress
decompress_direct worst as decompress

# Main-loop work that runs on into vblank after the NMI handler, writing RAM
# (tables and tile buffers the next frame uploads), not video registers: its
# error moves when the RAM is ready, and the calls are as long as vblank or
# longer, so the original's own time already runs them into the picture.
field_rasterize_quad vblank-tail rasterizes a window shape into a RAM table (35 lines a call, as long as vblank); 7.5% error, the time follows the quadrilateral's edges
field_rasterize_quad vblank-worst as for vblank-tail: at most 14885 clocks (11 lines) in a call of 35 lines or more, writing RAM
menu_object_wave_hdma vblank-tail fills a RAM scroll-wave table in 53-line calls, longer than vblank; its error never exceeds 1396 clocks (one line), just over the tail's 1024
menu_draw_glyph vblank-tail draws a glyph into the menu's tile buffer in RAM in 18-line calls; its time follows the glyph's pixels; at most 2658 clocks (two lines)
menu_draw_text vblank-tail draws text into the menu's tilemap buffer in RAM in 19-line calls; at most 4509 clocks (3.3 lines) in vblank
battle_draw_objects vblank-tail draws the battle objects into the OAM buffer in RAM in 37-line calls, longer than vblank; its time follows the objects' parts, effects and afterimages; 10.7% error
battle_draw_objects vblank-worst as for vblank-tail: at most 23882 clocks (18 lines) in a call of 37 lines on average, writing RAM
battle_draw_object_underlays vblank-tail part of battle_draw_objects' pass: an object's markers, number or effect sprites into the OAM buffer in RAM; its time follows which of the three it draws
battle_draw_object_underlays vblank-worst as for vblank-tail: at most 17409 clocks, inside a pass of 37 lines writing RAM
battle_step_effect_slots vblank-tail steps the 19 effect slots' paths in RAM (positions and the sine and arctangent products); its time follows which handlers run; 7.9% of calls in vblank off by a line or more
battle_step_effect_slots vblank-worst as for vblank-tail: at most 18133 clocks, writing RAM the object drawing reads later in the frame
battle_screen_wave_windows vblank-tail fills the two window line buffers in RAM in 94-line calls; 1.3% error, at most 4506 clocks
battle_step_hp_gradient vblank-tail writes three palette rows in RAM, uploaded at the next vblank; 1.58% of calls off by 1024 clocks or more, at most 2509
