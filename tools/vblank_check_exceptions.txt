# Accepted findings of tools/vblank_check.py (docs/RECOMP.md, "Where video writes land").
#
# One per line: routine group category reason...
#   routine   a hand port's name, or an address ($C0:1234 or C0:1234) for a port or generated block
#   group     display, oam, bg, vram, mode7, cgram, screens, hdma-enable, or * for any
#   category  visible, unblank or lines
#   reason    why the damage is accepted, in words (required)
#
# What may be accepted: a difference in where a write lands that is a line or two
# of picture on a few frames, and that can't change the game's outcome (a write
# to a display register, which nothing reads back to decide anything). The route
# check is what shows the outcome is unchanged. Anything larger, or anything that
# reaches game state, is a bug to fix, not an entry here.
#
